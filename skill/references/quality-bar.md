# Bravado review bar

- **Source truth:** URLs, branding, copy, resume facts, metrics, and availability reflect the linked project. No invented proof points or unlabeled illustrative balances.
- **Editorial clarity:** one dominant headline per scene; meaningful type scale; hierarchy still clear in a half-size preview; all text inside safe margins.
- **Motion meaning:** progressive charts and paths encode something real; packets, counters, arrows, and records have causes and destinations. Avoid decorative motion that conflicts with the claim.
- **Choreography:** a seven-scene arc with staggered headline reveals, accents on actual beats, designed transitions, and room for each idea to register.
- **Sound:** original SFX/score, balanced and synchronized; no clipping, jarring jumps, or accidental silence. Do not reuse reference audio.
- **Transitions:** inspect the outgoing frame, exact cut, mid-wipe, settled scene, and final frame; midpoint storyboards alone can hide flashes and clipped entrances.
- **Export:** review storyboard and full H.264/AAC MP4, 1920×1080/30 fps/31 seconds by default; decode through final frame. Provide editable sources.

If a source has no visual identity, create a coherent proposed direction and say it is a proposal. If a different structure serves the story, adapt the renderer and timing deliberately rather than forcing the sample sequence.
